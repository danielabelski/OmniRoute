### profaịlụ mkpakọ OmniGlyph

Injin `omniglyph` (ngwugwu `omniglyph`, 1.4.0 +) na-anabata profaịlụ nkọwa aha aha, nke edobere n'ụwa niile site na `omniglyph.profile` na ntọala mkpakọ ma ọ bụ kwa nzọụkwụ site na nhazi usoro pipeline.

| Profaịlụ      | Oke                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `aggressive`  | Ọdabara. Iwu nke nnata ebipụtara tụrụ usoro onyonyo —, dọkụmentị ngwaọrụ na akụkọ ihe mere eme siri ike    |
| `balanced`    | Na-eme ka steeti dị ndụ dị ka nwa afọ, na-echebe ntụgharị 8 ikpeazụ, daa akụkọ ihe mere eme mechiri emechi |
| `coding-safe` | Na-edobe ikike, schemas ngwá ọrụ na mmepụta ngwá ọrụ dị ndụ, na-echebe ntụgharị 12 ikpeazụ                 |
| `passthrough` | Ụzọ na-enweghị mgbanwe; awụliri igwe                                                                       |

Profaịlụ bụ a** uko ụlọ, ọ bụghị ala** : `mergeCompressionProfileOptions` n'ime ngwugwu jụrụ ikwe ka onye na-akpọ oku mebie ọzọ mechiri ụzọ na-efunahụ profaịlụ ahụ, yabụ `preserveSystemPrompt: false` nke ọ bụla enweghị ike ịmegharị mkpakọ sistemụ n'okpuru `coding-safe`.

Atụtara na codebase a: `coding-safe` na `balanced` na-ebuli `minCompressChars` ruo n'ókè ya ma debe usoro, atụmatụ ngwá ọrụ na nsonaazụ ngwá ọrụ, ya mere nnọkọ nke na-emebeghị akụkọ ihe mere eme na-akwụsị na `below_min_chars` na engine na-agbanwe ihe ọ bụla. Ọ bụ ya mere ndabara ji bụrụ `aggressive` karịa profaịlụ kacha dịrị nchebe.

Ngwungwu a na-edozi ụdị nke ya na profaịlụ site na nhazi gburugburu ya. OmniRoute anaghị ewepụta mkpebi ahụ: ihe nkwụnye ahụ na-atụnye ọnụ ụzọ ihe atụ ahụ n'ọnụ ụzọ kacha mma nke ngwugwu ahụ, yabụ ntọala gburugburu ebe obibi nwere ike belata naanị ndepụta ikike, ọ dịghị mgbe ọ gafechara nnata OmniRoute tụrụ atụ.

## Ndekọ engine

Ndebanye aha bi na `open-sse/services/compression/engines/registry.ts`. Injin na-ekpughe nkwekọrịta nkekọrịtara:

- `id`: id engine kwụsiri ike dị ka `caveman` ma ọ bụ `rtk`
- `apply(text, config)`: ụzọ mkpochapụ nke ọkpọkọ ọkpọkọ ji
- `compress(input, config)`: ụzọ mkpochapụ izizi na-eweghachi stats + ederede
- `getConfigSchema()`: na-eweghachi ụdị nhazi dị ka JSON-Schema
- `validateConfig(config)`: laghachi `{ valid, errors[] }`

Ndebanye aha na-eji `registerCompressionEngine(engine)` (ma ọ bụ `registerEngine` maka ikpe dị elu), nke na-akpọ `assertValidEngine()` na `validateConfig(defaultConfig)` tupu ịnakwere. Jiri `unregisterCompressionEngine(id)` wepụ engine n'oge ọ na-agba ọsọ.

`strategySelector.ts` na-edebanye aha igwe arụnyere n'ime ya tupu mkpakọ agbaa. Nke a na-ahapụ ịhụchalụ, mkpakọ oge ojiri gaa, ọnọdụ stacked, ule, na injin n'ọdịnihu jiri otu ụzọ ogbugbu.

### mkpakọ nkọwa MCP (metụtara)

Ndekọ dị iche na-akpakọ metadata nkọwa ngwa MCP na ọkwa ndekọ — hụ `open-sse/mcp-server/descriptionCompressor.ts` na [MCP-SERVER.md](../frameworks/MCP-SERVER.md). Ọ na-ejigharị iwu Caveman mana ọ na-arụ ọrụ na metadata ngwaọrụ, ọ bụghị ịrịọ maka ibu ọrụ.

### Igwe arụnyere n'ime ya

N'ofe Caveman, RTK, na LLMLingua-2, ụgbọ mmiri ndị na-edebanye aha na-ebuga ọtụtụ engines na-enweghị ihe ọ bụla pụrụ iche (nke a na-eji pipeline, ebe egwuregwu na ule):

| Injin    | Id              | Ihe ọ na-eme                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CCR      | `ccr`           | Content-Compress-Retrieve (H4): na-eji ntụnyere ọdịnaya na-edochi nnukwu ngọngọ ederede na-aga n'ihu, ya mere a na-eziga repeated/large blocks otu ugboro wee degharịa ya ma emechaa. |
| isi ụlọ  | `headroom`      | SmartCrusher (H3 + N5): mkpirisi tabular na-enweghị atụ nke nrụnye ụgwọ ọrụ JSON-array n'ime ụdị `[N rows]` kọlụm.                                                                    |
| ionizer  | `ionizer`       | nlebanya ahịrị Head/middle/tail maka nnukwu ihe jikọrọ ọnụ, na-echekwa etiti kpuchiri ekpuchi ka ọ bụrụ nrụtụ aka nwere ọdịnaya CCR.                                                  |
| oge-deup | `session-dedup` | Mwepụ ntugharị n'ofe nke a na-agwa ọdịnaya (TokenMizer-sitere n'ike mmụọ nsọ): na-ewepụ ederede ahụrụla na ntụgharị mbụ nke otu nnọkọ.                                                |

**CCR eweghachi-ntụziaka protocol (#8033):** oge mbụ CCR na-anọchi ≥ 1 ngọngọ na arịrịọ, engine na-akwadebe otu ozi `system` siri ike (na-eduga na `[CCR protocol]` sentinel) na-akụziri onye na-akpọ onye na-akpọ akara → ngwá ọrụ nkwekọrịta: ihe akara `[CCR retrieve hash=<24hex> chars=N]` pụtara, na a ghaghị depụtaghachi hash verbatim (ihe niile 24 hex characters — mis-copied a hashes `[dedup:ref sha=...]` akara pụtara "leghachi azụ n'akụkọ ihe mere eme", ọ bụghị "ịkpọ ngwá ọrụ". A na-agbanye ndetu ahụ** naanị mgbe `tools[]` onye na-akpọ oku na-akpọsa gosipụtara na ọ nwere ike iru `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` n'ezie** (`callerSupportsCcrRetrieve()` in `open-sse/services/compression/engines/ccr/protocolInstruction.ts`) — onye na-akpọ OpenAI dakọtara na enweghị ngwa ahụ anaghị enweta ntụziaka ịkpọ ihe ọ na-enweghị ike iru. A na-amanye ikike ikike site na inyocha akụkọ ihe mere eme nke sentinel tupu ịgbanye, ya mere arịrịọ ntụgharị ọtụtụ (nke na-emegharị ozi mbụ) anaghị etinye akwụkwọ ndetu otu ugboro n'otu oge.

## onye caveman

Ụdị Caveman na-elekwasị anya na condensation semantic nke prose nkịtị:

- na-echekwa ngọngọ koodu, URLs, JSON, ụzọ na data ahaziri
- na-ewepụ ihe ndochi, mgbochi, ihe gbara ya gburugburu, na nkebiokwu njikọ ọnụ
- na-akwado ngwugwu iwu faịlụ maara asụsụ na `open-sse/services/compression/rules/`
- ka dị site na ụdị `standard`, `aggressive` na `ultra`

Elu dashboard bụ `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> Caveman`.

Caveman upstream na-akọ `~75%` obere mmepụta ihe, `65%` nkezi mmepụta ego na benchmark nwere oke `22-87%`, yana ngwa ntinye ntinye `~46%`. OmniRoute na-eji nọmba ntinye-akụkụ Caveman mgbe ị na-edekọ nchekwa ego prompt/context ; Ụdị mmepụta Caveman na-anọgide na-abụ njirimara nzaghachi-omume dị iche.

## RTK

- na-achọpụta klaasị mmepụta dị ka `git status`, `git branch`, `git diff`, Vitest/Jest/Pytest, Cargo/Go ule , TypeScript/Vite/Webpack na-ewuli , ESLint, npm audit/installs, Docker logs , shei `find`/`grep`, stack traces , na generic logs

- ibe ANSI usoro, mkpọtụ na-aga n'ihu, ahịrị ugboro ugboro, na efere esi mmiri na-adịghị enye aka
- na-echekwa ọdịda enwere ike ime, ịdọ aka ná ntị, nchịkọta, faịlụ gbanwere, na ọdụ ọdụ
- nwere ike na-ahọrọ ijido rected raw mmepụta maka recovery/debugging na ezi ụzọ njikwa

## LLMLingua-2 (Semantic kwachaa)

LLMLingua-2 mode na-arụ ọrụ** semantic token kwachaa** na prose na-eji obere ọkwa ọkwa ONNX, na-emeju injin Caveman na RTK dabere na iwu:

- na-akpakọ prose na ozi na-abụghị sistemụ naanị; A naghị agbanwe ngọngọ koodu gbara agba na ihe ndị ọzọ echekwara
- na-agba azụ azụ `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` (ONNX via `@huggingface/transformers`) na eriri onye ọrụ, yabụ ntinye ihe atụ anaghị egbochi loop mmemme arịrịọ.
- bụ** stackable** (`stackPriority` 35): n'ime pipeline a chịkọtara, ọ na-aga n'azụ engines nhazi (CCR, session-dedup, headroom, Caveman) ma tupu `ultra`, ebe ọ bụ na semantic kwachaa kacha dị irè na ugbua-nhazi-mmetụta ederede — eg `rtk -> caveman -> llmlingua`
- **ọdịda-emepe na njehie ọ bụla** (nhọrọ nhọrọ na-efu efu, spawn onye ọrụ, ibu ihe nlereanya, ntinye aka, ma ọ bụ oge nkwụsị) → eweghachite ederede mbụ na-agbanweghị agbanwe, ọ nweghịkwa njehie.

Ebe injin: `open-sse/services/compression/engines/llmlingua/`. Elu dashboard bụ `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> LLMLingua`.

### Ụdị

Ụdị ndabara bụ**TinyBERT** (`atjsh/llmlingua-2-js-tinybert-meetingbank`, ~ 57 MB, ngwa ngwa). A elu-nkenke** BERT-base** nlereanya (`Arcoldd/llmlingua4j-bert-base-onnx`, ~ 710 MB) dị site na engine config `model` ubi. `@huggingface/transformers` na-ebudata ụdị ahọpụtara n'ime umengwụ site na HuggingFace Hub n'ime `${DATA_DIR}/models/llmlingua` na oku izizi (`modelStore.ts`); Nhazi `modelPath` na-atụ aka ya na nnomi mpaghara kama (nwụnye na-anọghị n'ịntanetị / ikuku nwere oghere).

### Ndabere na nhọrọ & wụnye ihe achọrọ

Ngwunye ndị ọgbọ LLMLingua a na-egbutu ya bụ** nhọrọ** . A na-ekwupụta ngwugwu abụọ dị ka `optionalDependencies` na `package.json` ma debe ya** mpụga** site na mmepụta mmepụta (`scripts/build/prepublish.ts` anaghị ejikọta ha):

| ngwugwu              | Ụdị (pin) | Ihe ndetu                                          |
| -------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` | `2.0.5`   | ngwugwu ntinye; na-ekwupụta ndị ọzọ dị ka ndị ọgbọ |
| `js-tiktoken`        | `^1.0.20` | Tokenizer                                          |

A na-atụnye `@huggingface/transformers` na `^4.2.0` (nke a na-ekekọrịta ya na ụzọ ntinye mpaghara yana achọpụtara ya na ngwugwu kwụ ọtọ); `@atjsh/llmlingua-2@2.0.5` ndị ọgbọ na ya na `"^3.5.2 || ^4.0.0"`, yabụ a na-akwado ma Transformers.js v3 na v4. Ebe ọ bụ na 2.0.4, `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` anaghịzi achọ `@tensorflow/tfjs`, nke wepụrụ nnukwu otu onye ntinye aka (TensorFlow.js) na nchịkọta SLM. Naanị ngwungwu abụọ dị n'elu bụ ndị ọgbọ SLM a na-egbutu ya. Ọkọlọtọ `npm install` (dev) na-etinye nchịkọta nhọrọ na-akpaghị aka ma ọ bụrụ na ahapụghị ihe ndabere nhọrọ.

**Ihe kpatara na-achọ:** ngwugwu nke ebipụtara npm, ùkwù guzoro ọtọ, na ụgbọ ihe oyiyi Docker** na-enweghị** ndị a deps na-anọ ebughibu. Mgbe ha na-anọghị, ọnụ ụzọ adabere na onye ọrụ (a `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` resolve probe in `worker.ts`) daa na engine.** ada-emepe nkiti** — na-ahọpụta LLMLingua na-aghọ no-op (ederede eweghachiri agbanweghị, enweghị mperi abanyela). Iji mee ka ọ rụọ ọrụ n'ebe a kpachara, wụnye nchịkọta nhọrọ:

```bash
# pin to the versions declared in package.json optionalDependencies
npm install @atjsh/llmlingua-2@2.0.5 js-tiktoken
```

Mwepụ `@tensorflow/tfjs` (2.0.4 +) na-ewepụ ~ 800 MB onye na-enye aka na mbụ — akara ụkwụ fọdụrụnụ bụ transformers.js + onnxruntime-node runtimes, gbakwunyere TinyBERT ụdị (~ 57 MB) ebudatara na mbụ ojiji (ọ bụghị site npm).

Kwa gburugburu:

- **Dev / `npm install`** — arụnyere na-akpaghị aka ma ọ bụrụ na ị gafere `--omit=optional` (ma ọ bụ `--no-optional`). Ọ nweghị ihe achọrọ.

## Pipeline tokọrọ

Ụdị akwakọbara na-eme usoro pipeline n'usoro. Ihe ndabara bụ:

```txt
rtk -> caveman
```

Jiri nke a maka nnọkọ onye nnọchite koodu ebe ngwa ngwa na-ejikọta mmepụta iwu na usoro mmadụ ma ọ bụ onye enyemaka. RTK na-ebu ụzọ belata ndekọ ngwá ọrụ mkpọtụ, mgbe ahụ Caveman na-akpakọ asụsụ eke fọdụrụnụ.

A na-ahazi usoro pipeline na `stackedPipeline` na ntọala mkpakọ ma ọ bụ site na combos mkpakọ.

Mgbe engines abụọ ahụ belatara otu ụgwọ ọrụ tozuru oke, mkpokọta ego:

```txt
combined = 1 - (1 - RTK savings) * (1 - Caveman input savings)
average  = 1 - (1 - 0.80) * (1 - 0.46) = 89.2%
range    = 1 - (1 - 0.60..0.90) * (1 - 0.46) = 78.4-94.6%
```

## MCP Ịnweta Osisi Filter

Ihe nzacha nweta MCP -osisi smart filter bụ oyi akwa mkpakọ gachara nke na-agba na MCP** nsonaazụ ngwá ọrụ** , ọ bụghị na mkpali ma ọ bụ gburugburu. Ọ na-elekwasị anya ịnweta verbose-osisi na ihe nchọgharị ihe nchọgharị na-akwụ ụgwọ nke ngwaọrụ dị ka Playwright, iji kọmputa na ihe nchọgharị-akpaaka MCP.

### Ihe ọ na-eme

1. **Ịwepụ mkpọtụ** — na-ewepụ ndenye generic/text efu (`- generic:`, `- text: ""`)
2. **Ụmụnne dara** — mgbe ≥ `collapseThreshold` (ndabere 30) ahịrị ndị dị n'usoro bụ ihe nhazigharị, na-adaba n'ime `collapseKeepHead` nke mbụ (ndabere 10) ahịrị + nchịkọta nchịkọta + ikpeazụ `collapseKeepTail` (ndabere 5) ahịrị.
3. **Nchekwa ref** — `[ref=eXX]` arịlịka chọrọ nke Playwright/computer-use anaghị emetụ ya aka
4. **Ọkpụkpụ siri ike** — ma ọ bụrụ na ederede ka ọ daa ka gafere `maxTextChars` (50,000 ndabara), mkpọmkpọ ebe nwere akara igodo ka onye ọrụ ahụ wee gaa n'ihu na-arụ ọrụ

### Ebe engine

```txt
open-sse/services/compression/engines/mcpAccessibility/
  index.ts            ← smartFilterText() entry point
  collapseRepeated.ts ← sibling-collapse algorithm
  constants.ts        ← DEFAULT_MCP_ACCESSIBILITY_CONFIG
```

### Nhazi

`compression.mcpAccessibility` na-achịkwa ya na ntọala ụwa (mbugharị 056). Nhazi izizi:

```json
{
  "enabled": true,
  "maxTextChars": 50000,
  "collapseThreshold": 30,
  "collapseKeepHead": 10,
  "collapseKeepTail": 5,
  "minLengthToProcess": 2000
}
```

A na-etinye nzacha ahụ naanị na nrụnye nrụpụta akụrụngwa nke `type` bụ `"text"` na ogologo ya karịrị `minLengthToProcess`. Ọ naghị emetụta mkpakọ ngwa ngwa ma ọ bụ rịọ ka a kwụọ ụgwọ.

### Nchekwa tụrụ anya

60–80% na nsonaazụ ngwa ihe onyonyo ihe nchọgharị, dabere na mgbagwoju ibe. Algọridim ọdịda ahụ bụ O(n) n'ọnụọgụ ahịrị ma na-agbakwụnye latency na-adịghị emetụta.

Nkebi a bụ nrụtụ aka nke onye na-arụ ọrụ maka ijikọ onye ahịa elu, na-agụ akara abụọ, na iweghachite ma ọ bụ chọpụta ọdịnaya ekpuchirila. A na-ekpuchi ígwè ọrụ nke injin ọ bụla n'elu; Nke a bụ oyi akwa "kedu ka m ga-esi agba ya".

### Nkwakọba ọkwa (ihe na-adị ndụ ma malitegharịa)

CCR bu a** okpukpu abụọ** ụlọ ahịa, ọ bụghị naanị na ebe nchekwa:

- **Ọkwa ebe nchekwa dị ọkụ** — kwa ngọngọ okpu 2 MiB, kwa onye isi 16 MiB, zuru ụwa ọnụ 64 MiB. Ndenye ndabara na 24 h TTL (oke ụbọchị 7). Mweghachi MCP zuru ezu site na ebe nchekwa na-ekpuchi na 256 KiB; A ka na-enweta ihe ọ bụla buru ibu site na ụdịdị grep/grep.
- **Tier SQLite na-adịgide adịgide** (`ccr_blocks`, `COMPRESSION_CCR_DURABLE_STORE`, na ndabara) — na-akwado ọkwa in-ncheta ka ngọngọ na-adị ndụ LRU nchụpụ, ịmalitegharịa, ma ọ bụ weghachite ọdịda na oge ọzọ. Ahịrị ahụ na-edobe TTL ngọngọ ahụ, yabụ ngọngọ gafere TTL ya pụọ na ọkwa abụọ ahụ.

Ndị nche atọ na-edobe ọkwa na-adịgide adịgide — ihe mgbochi na-edobe ebe nchekwa-naanị mgbe:

1. ihe mgbochi ahụ agwụla** 512 KB** (`MAX_DURABLE_BLOCK_BYTES`);
2. oge ịgba ọsọ bụ oge ịgba ọsọ ígwé ojii (enweghị diski mpaghara);
3. `COMPRESSION_CCR_DURABLE_STORE=false`.

Ya mere, "ngbochi ahụ kwụsịrị" abụghị akụkọ mmalite mmalite: ihe mgbochi sub-512 KB na mpaghara, oge na-adịghị na ígwé ojii na-adị ndụ ma malitegharịa site na ọkwa na-adịgide adịgide ruo mgbe TTL ya ga-agwụ. Lee [ntụaka gburugburu](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) maka mgbanwe.

### Ihe nrịbama abụọ ahụ

| Ihe nrịbama                           | Pụtara                                                                                                                                | Ihe nlereanya kwesịrị ime                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Nke a bụ ihe** ụma ele anya azụ** , ọ bụghị mfu ma ọ bụ njehie. | Mee** ọ bụghị** kpọọ ngwá ọrụ ọ bụla ma mee** ọ bụghị** gbalịa 'ịrụgharịa' ya. Ihe omume mbụ nke ederede bụ akwụkwọ edemede; A naghị edegharị ntụgharị nke ugbu a, a na-ewepụ ya na ntụgharị ọzọ. |

### Ihe ndị ahịa chọrọ

- **Igodo (stdio):** onye na-akpọ stdio anaghị eburu njirimara onye ọ bụla, yabụ ọ na-eji `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` gosi ya (nke edobere na `env` nkesa MCP ). Na-enweghị ya, onye na-akpọ oku enweghị aha yana ngwaọrụ ndị chọrọ onye isi ụlọ akwụkwọ nwere ike weda ya ala.
- **`tools[]` bụ ntụ ntụ ntụ.** A na-agbaba ntuziaka CCR eweghachi-protocol (#8033).** naanị** mgbe `tools[]` kpọsara arịrịọ ahụ gosipụtara na onye ahịa nwere ike iru `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` n'ezie. Egwuregwu a bụ egwuregwu nzọ ụkwụ nke onye nkewa ga-eke (`__`, `.`, `/`, `:`) — enwebeghị eriri obere — nke mere na aha enweghị aha dịka `mcp__gateway__omniroute_ccr_retrieve` ka na-agụ. `tools[]` na-anọghị ma ọ bụ nke na-adakọghị pụtara na agbanyeghị ntuziaka _not_, site na imewe.
- **`codemode:false` (ndị ahịa OpenCode ).** OpenCode na-adabara na Usoro Koodu, nke na-akpọsa naanị ngwa ala ya na ihe nlereanya yana** adịghị** tinye ngwa MCP na ndepụta `tools[]` nke ala. Tọọ `codemode: false` na ntinye nkesa MCP ka akpọsa ngwá ọrụ `omniroute_ccr_*`. Nke a bụ a** onye ahịa** ntọala, ọ bụghị otu OmniRoute. Ọ nwetaghị ntụziaka CCR ahụ: OpenCode na-akpọ ngwá ọrụ `<server>_<tool>`, ya mere sava a na-akpọ `omniroute` na-ekpughe `omniroute_omniroute_ccr_retrieve`, na `_` abụghị otu n'ime ndị na-ekewapụta ya.
- **Ndị isi.** Nchekwa na iweghachi dịpụrụ adịpụ maka onye isi ụlọ akwụkwọ (kwa igodo API ). Igodo na-ahụ naanị ngọngọ nke ya.

### Oke iweghachi & pagination

A na-ebibiri nsonaazụ ederede MCP gburugburu `mcpAccessibility.maxTextChars` ( `50000` ndabara). Mweghachi `full` nke ngọngọ bụ 256 KiB —** enweghị oke iweghachite n'uju** . Mgbe `chars=N` ngọngọ buru ibu, depụta ya kama ịdọrọ ihe niile:

- Iwu nke isi mkpịsị aka:**`chars > 40000` → jiri a `mode`** , ọ dịghị mgbe iferi juru.
- `mode:"grep"` — regex `pattern` dabara n'ahịrị n'ahịrị, ruo 1,000 ahịrị dakọtara (`unique: true` drops repeats), dị ọnụ ala maka "chọta otu ahịrị m chọrọ."
- `mode:"lines"` — akara ahịrị 1-indexed; chọrọ ma `start` na `end`.
- `mode:"head"` / `mode:"tail"` — first/last `n` ahịrị.
- `mode:"stats"` — ahịrị, agwa, na byte na-agụta na-enweghị ahụ.

Jiri `mode:"lines"` jiri `start`/`end` gaa ije ogologo ngọngọ na ibe kama ịrịọ ihe niile ozugbo.

### Ịkpọ aha ngwá ọrụ

A na-akpọ ngwá ọrụ eweghachite ugbu a `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` (ngọnarị `omniroute_` bụ ihe arụrụ arụ ụbọchị maka ndakọrịta aha ihe nkesa). Aha ngwa suffix nwere oke dị ọcha yana ndozi maka nsonaazụ MCP JSON mkpirisi etiti eriri ka a na-esochi ihe nleba anya elu; ibe a na-edekọ omume ugbu a, naanị mbupu.

### okirikiri ndụ nke oge

Streamable-HTTP sessions na-abaghị uru ma ihe nkesa na-aghọrọ ha; Window na-abaghị uru bụ `MCP_SESSION_IDLE_MS` na-adịgide adịgide (nke a na-akpọ aha, ọ bụghị uru nwere koodu siri ike, ya mere ibe a anaghị agbagharị mgbe ọ gbanwere). Ihe mgbaàmà nke nnọkọ a na-eweghị ihe ubi bụ HTTP 404 nwere `Unknown Mcp-Session-Id` — ọgwụgwọ bụ ijikọ ma ọ bụ malitegharịa nnọkọ ahụ,** ọ bụghị** ka ịtụgharị igodo, gbanwee oghere, ma ọ bụ nyegharịa ngwaọrụ aha.

## Oke & mwepu

**A naghị ejikọta ihe mgbakwunye.** `open-sse/handlers/embeddings.ts` anaghị akpọ engine mkpakọ ọ bụla — ahụ request/response na-agafe ozugbo na onye mmetu ahụ emetụghị ya aka. Nke a bụ ihe arụpụtara taa (ntinye na mmecha mkparịta ụka bụ ndị na-arụkọ ọrụ ọnụ), ọ bụghị nlele oge ojiri, mana ọ pụtara na nchegbu vector-distortion na #8034 enweghị mkpughe elu n'ụzọ ntinye.

**nzacha mwepu Per-model/endpoint (#8034).** Maka mmecha nkata, onye na-arụ ọrụ nwere ike ịkpọ ụdị ids / `provider/model` lekwasịrị anya nke a na-agaghị agbanye — ụzọ nchekwa bara uru ma ọ bụrụ na mkpakọ na-adịru nso na ụzọ ntinye-n'akụkụ ma emechaa, yana bara uru maka ụdị ọ bụla nke kpọmkwem byte-for-byte ozugbo (deterministic evals, cache-sensitive prefixes, wdg).

- Ogige ntọala: `exclusions?: string[]` na nhazi mkpakọ zuru ụwa ọnụ (`GET`/`PUT /api/settings/compression`), nọgidere na-esi na `key_value` mkpakọ namespace dị ugbu a (`src/lib/db/compression.ts`) — enweghị tebụl ọhụrụ.
- Taabụ dashboard:** Dashboard → Mkpakọ → Mwepu** (`/dashboard/compression/exclusions`).
- Nhazi usoro: `*` bụ sọọsọ egwu egwu. Metacharacter regex ọ bụla ọzọ na ụkpụrụ na-agbanarị tupu ọ dabara, yabụ `gpt-5.6` dabara naanị eriri nkịtị, ọ dịghị mgbe `gpt-5x6` (ReDoS-safe, bounded, nosted quantifiers). Ụkpụrụ dabara na ikpe-enweghị mgbagha megide ma ụdị id efu yana `provider/model` mejupụtara — `gpt-5-6`, `openai/gpt-5-6`, na `openai/*` ọrụ niile, na `*` naanị na-ewepu ụdị ọ bụla.
- Dakọtara: `isCompressionExcluded()` / `normalizeCompressionExclusions()` na `open-sse/services/compression/exclusions.ts`. `chatCore.ts` na-enyocha ebumnuche ewepụrụ ozugbo emechara ntọala mkpakọ,** tupu engine ọ bụla agbaa** , ma na-emeso egwuregwu kpọmkwem dị ka mkpakọ nwere nkwarụ zuru ụwa ọnụ — ahụ arịrịọ ahụ bụ ihe na-egosi na ọ bụ byte. A na-edekọ ihe nfe ahụ site na `writeCompressionSkip(..., "excluded")` maka ọhụhụ nyocha.
- Ndi an-kpọ (ndepụta empty/absent ): yiri omume pre-#8034 — ọ nweghị ihe ewepụrụ.

## Amaghị oke

- **LLMLingua-2 (SLM) chọrọ deps nhọrọ ndị jikọtara ọnụ.** Onye ọrụ ahụ na-agba ọsọ naanị n'ime nrụpụta mgbe ndị ọgbọ `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` + nọ n'ime `dist/node_modules` (lee `scripts/build/colocateOptionals.mjs`, #4286). Na-enweghị ha, injin ahụ ga-emepe (na-eweghachi ederede mbụ). Mkpebi onye ọrụ anaghị adabere na `import.meta.url` (ọ na-anwụ na ngwugwu kwụ ọtọ) — ọ na-akwado na oge ịgba ọsọ cwd / `argv[1]`.
- **Ngwungwu asụsụ Caveman `de` / `fr` / `ja` bụ akụkụ.** Ha na-ebuga `context` + `filler` + `structural` iwu mana ọ nweghị ngwugwu `dedup` / `ultra`, yabụ ike `ultra` esighi ike karịa `full` maka asụsụ ndị ahụ (ha na-eji naanị iwu nke aka ha — ọ nweghị ihe ọ bụla dara ada azụ na iwu `dedup`/`ultra` nke bekee, nke ga-agbakọ ederede mba ọzọ). `en` / `es` / `id` / `pt-BR` zuru ezu. A na-anabata onyinye `dedup.json` + `ultra.json` maka ngwugwu akụkụ.
- **Telemetry stacked na-edepụta naanị injin ndị abịakọrọ.** Nzọụkwụ ọkpọkọ kpọkọtara nke injin ya na-agba ọsọ mana ọ rụpụtara 0% ego na-eweghachi `stats:null`, yabụ na ọ pụtaghị na `engineBreakdown` — a na-apụghị ịmata site na nzọụkwụ nke awụsara. Ịmata ọdịiche "na-agba ọsọ, 0 %" na "awụsara" ga-achọ mgbanwe nhụsianya-mgbanwe ma echere ya.
