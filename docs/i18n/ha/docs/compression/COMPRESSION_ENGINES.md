### OmniGlyph matsawa profiles

Injin `omniglyph` (kunshin `omniglyph`, 1.4.0 +) yana karɓar bayanin martaba mai suna, wanda aka saita a duniya ta hanyar `omniglyph.profile` a cikin saitunan matsawa ko kowane mataki ta hanyar daidaita matakan bututun:

| Bayanan martaba | Iyaka                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `aggressive`    | Default Manufar rasidun da aka buga sun auna tsarin hotunan —, takaddun kayan aiki da tarihin mai yawa      |
| `balanced`      | Yana riƙe raye-rayen ɗan asalin jihar, yana kare jujjuyawar 8 na ƙarshe, ya rushe tsohon tarihin rufaffiyar |
| `coding-safe`   | Yana riƙe iko, tsare-tsaren kayan aiki da fitowar kayan aiki kai tsaye, yana kare jujjuyawar 12 na ƙarshe   |
| `passthrough`   | Hanyoyi ba tare da canzawa ba; an tsallake injin                                                            |

Profile shine a** rufi, ba bene** : `mergeCompressionProfileOptions` a cikin kunshin ya ƙi barin mai kira ya soke ya sake buɗe layin da aka yi asara bayanin martaba ya rufe, don haka kowane mataki `preserveSystemPrompt: false` ba zai iya sake kunna tsarin matsawa a ƙarƙashin `coding-safe`.

An auna akan wannan codebase: `coding-safe` da `balanced` suna ɗaga `minCompressChars` zuwa iyakarsa kuma su kiyaye tsarin, tsare-tsaren kayan aiki da sakamakon kayan aiki na asali, don haka zaman da bai tara tarihi ba ya tsaya a `below_min_chars` kuma injin ɗin bai canza komai ba. Shi yasa tsohowar shine `aggressive` maimakon bayanin martaba mafi aminci.

Kunshin yana warware girman samfurin sa da bayanin martaba daga tsarin yanayin sa. OmniRoute ba ta taɓa ƙaddamar da shawarar ba: adaftan yana haɗa ƙofar ƙirar zuwa mafi ƙanƙantar fakitin, don haka saitunan mahalli na iya taƙaita jerin izini kawai, ba za su taɓa faɗaɗa shi sama da ƙimar OmniRoute ba.

## Injin Registry

Wurin yin rajista yana zaune a cikin `open-sse/services/compression/engines/registry.ts`. Injiniya suna fallasa kwangilar da aka raba:

- `id`: tabbatattun injin id kamar `caveman` ko `rtk`
- `apply(text, config)`: hanyar aiwatar da gadon da aka yi amfani da shi ta hanyar toshe bututun mai
- `compress(input, config)`: hanyar aiwatarwa ta farko ta dawo da kididdigar +
- `getConfigSchema()`: yana dawo da sifar JSON-Schema mai inganci mai inganci
- `validateConfig(config)`: dawo `{ valid, errors[] }`

Rijista yana amfani da `registerCompressionEngine(engine)` (ko `registerEngine` don shari'o'in ci gaba), wanda ke kiran `assertValidEngine()` da `validateConfig(defaultConfig)` kafin karɓa. Yi amfani da `unregisterCompressionEngine(id)` don cire inji a lokacin aiki.

`strategySelector.ts` tana yin rijistar ginanniyar injuna kafin matsawa ta gudana. Wannan yana ba da damar samfoti, matsawar lokacin aiki, yanayin da aka tattara, gwaje-gwaje, da injunan gaba su yi amfani da hanyar aiwatarwa iri ɗaya.

### matsawa bayanin MCP (mai alaƙa)

Wurin yin rajista daban yana matsawa MCP bayanin kayan aikin metadata a matakin-rejista — duba `open-sse/mcp-server/descriptionCompressor.ts` da [MCP-SERVER.md](../frameworks/MCP-SERVER.md). Yana sake amfani da ƙa'idodin Caveman amma yana aiki akan metadata na kayan aiki, ba neman lodin biya ba.

### Ƙarin ginanniyar injuna

Bayan Caveman, RTK, da LLMLingua-2, rajistan yana jigilar injunan asara da yawa na musamman (ana amfani da bututun da aka toshe, filin wasa, da gwaje-gwaje):

| Injin       | Id              | Abin da yake yi                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CCR         | `ccr`           | Abun ciki-Damfara-Dawowa (H4): yana maye gurbin manyan tubalan rubutu masu jujjuyawa tare da abubuwan da aka gabatar da abun ciki, don haka ana aika tubalan repeated/large sau ɗaya sannan a bi da su daga baya. |
| dakin kai   | `headroom`      | SmartCrusher (H3 + N5): rashin asara tambura tara kayan aikin JSON-array mai kama da juna a cikin nau'in `[N rows]` na shafi.                                                                                     |
| ionizer     | `ionizer`       | Samfurin jeri Head/middle/tail don manyan tubalan kamanni, tana adana tsakiyar rufaffiyar azaman bayanin abin da ke cikin CCR.                                                                                    |
| zaman-tsare | `session-dedup` | Rarraba juzu'i-nau'i-nauyi (TokenMizer-wahayi): yana kawar da rubutun da aka riga aka gani a farkon juyi na wannan zama.                                                                                          |

**umarni na dawo da CCR (#8033):** A karo na farko CCR ya maye gurbin ≥ 1 block a cikin buƙatun, injin yana tsara saƙo guda ɗaya, idempotent `system` (jagora tare da `[CCR protocol]` sentinel) yana koya wa mai kira alamar → kayan aiki: abin da alamar `[CCR retrieve hash=<24hex> chars=N]` ke nufi, cewa za a yi kwafin hash verbatim (duk 24 hex characters — ba za a iya kwafin hashes) ba. alama `[dedup:ref sha=...]` nufin "duba tarihi", ba "kira kayan aiki ba". An yi allurar bayanin kula** kawai lokacin da mai kiran ya yi tallan `tools[]` ya tabbatar zai iya kaiwa `omniroute_ccr_retrieve`** (`callerSupportsCcrRetrieve()` in `open-sse/services/compression/engines/ccr/protocolInstruction.ts`) — a sarari mai buɗaɗɗen kira mai jituwa tare da kayan aikin ba tare da samun umarnin kiran wani abu da ba zai iya kaiwa ba. Ana aiwatar da rashin ƙarfi ta hanyar bincika tarihin saƙo na ma'aikaci kafin allura, don haka buƙatun-juyawa (waɗanda suke maimaita saƙon da suka gabata) ba sa tara bayanin sau ɗaya a kowane biki.

## Caveman

Yanayin Caveman yana mai da hankali kan ƙayyadaddun kalmomi na al'ada:

- yana adana tubalan lambar, URLs, JSON, hanyoyi, da bayanan da aka tsara
- yana cire filler, shinge, maimaita mahallin, da kalmomin haɗin kai
- yana goyan bayan fakitin dokokin fayil na sane da harshe a cikin `open-sse/services/compression/rules/`
- ya kasance ta hanyar gadon `standard`, `aggressive`, da `ultra` halaye

Fagen dashboard shine `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> Caveman`.

Caveman na sama yana ba da rahoton `~75%` abubuwan fitarwa, `65%` matsakaicin tanadin fitarwa a cikin maƙasudai tare da kewayon `22-87%`, da kayan aikin shigar-zurfin `~46%`. OmniRoute yana amfani da lambar shigar-gefen Caveman lokacin da ake tattara tarin ajiyar prompt/context ; Yanayin fitarwa na Caveman ya kasance nau'in fasalin amsa-halayen daban.

## LLMLingua-2 (Pruning Semantic)

yanayin LLMLingua-2 yayi** alamar ma'anar pruning** akan ilimantarwa ta amfani da ƙaramin rabe-raben alama ONNX, mai haɗa ƙa'idar tushen Caveman da injunan RTK :

- yana damfara rubutu a cikin saƙonnin da ba na tsarin ba kawai; Tubalan lambobin da aka katange da sauran abubuwan da aka adana ba su taɓa canzawa ba
- yana gudanar da bayanan baya `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` (ONNX via `@huggingface/transformers`) a cikin zaren ma'aikaci, don haka ƙirar ƙira ba ta toshe madaidaicin taron buƙatun
- shine** m** (`stackPriority` 35): a cikin bututun da aka tara yana gudana bayan injiniyoyin tsarin (CCR, session-dedup, headroom, Caveman) amma kafin `ultra`, tun da yake datsa ya fi tasiri akan riga-kafin-tsarin rubutu — misali `rtk -> caveman -> llmlingua`
- **kasa-bude kan kowane kuskure** (bacewar zaɓi na zaɓi, ma'aikaci, nauyin ƙira, ƙididdigewa, ko ƙarewar lokaci) → an dawo da ainihin rubutun baya canzawa, ba kuskure ba.

Wurin injin: `open-sse/services/compression/engines/llmlingua/`. Fagen dashboard shine `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> LLMLingua`.

### Samfura

Tsohuwar samfurin shine**TinyBERT** (`atjsh/llmlingua-2-js-tinybert-meetingbank`, ~ 57 MB, azumi). A mafi girma-daidaituwa** Farashin BERT** samfurin (`Arcoldd/llmlingua4j-bert-base-onnx`, ~ 710 MB) yana samuwa ta hanyar injin daidaita filin `model`. `@huggingface/transformers` yana sauke samfurin da aka zaɓa a kasala daga HuggingFace Hub zuwa `${DATA_DIR}/models/llmlingua` akan kiran farko (`modelStore.ts`); saitin `modelPath` ya soke yana nuna shi a kwafin gida maimakon (sakawar layi / iska mai tsafta).

### Abubuwan dogaro na zaɓi & shigarwa akan buƙata

The prunable LLMLingua runtime tari ne** na zaɓi** . An bayyana fakiti biyu a matsayin `optionalDependencies` a cikin `package.json` kuma an adana su** na waje** ta hanyar ginin samarwa (`scripts/build/prepublish.ts` baya haɗa su):

| Kunshin              | Sigar (pin) | Bayanan kula                                            |
| -------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` | `2.0.5`     | Kunshin shigarwa; bayyana sauran a matsayin takwarorina |
| `js-tiktoken`        | `^1.0.20`   | Tokenizer                                               |

Ana liƙa `@huggingface/transformers` a `^4.2.0` (an raba tare da hanyar haɗaɗɗen gida kuma ana binne shi cikin gunkin da aka keɓe); `@atjsh/llmlingua-2@2.0.5` takwarorinsa akan sa tare da `"^3.5.2 || ^4.0.0"`, don haka duka Transformers.js v3 da v4 ana tallafawa. Tun daga 2.0.4, `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` baya buƙatar `@tensorflow/tfjs`, wanda ya cire mafi girman mai ba da gudummawa ɗaya (TensorFlow.js) daga tarin SLM. Fakiti biyun da ke sama ne kawai takwarorinsu SLM da za a iya yankewa. Madaidaicin `npm install` (dev) yana shigar da tarin zaɓi ta atomatik sai dai idan ba a bar abin dogaro na zaɓi ba.

**Me yasa ake buƙata:** fakitin da aka buga npm, dam ɗin tsaye, da jirgin hoton Docker** ba tare da** wadannan deps zauna slim. Lokacin da ba su nan, ƙofar dogara ma ma'aikaci (bincike na `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` a cikin `worker.ts`) ya kasa kuma injin** kasa-bude shiru** — zabar LLMLingua ya zama no-op (rubutu da aka dawo baya canzawa, ba a shigar da kuskure ba). Don kunna shi a cikin wurin da aka datse, shigar da tari na zaɓi:

```bash
# pin to the versions declared in package.json optionalDependencies
npm install @atjsh/llmlingua-2@2.0.5 js-tiktoken
```

Cire `@tensorflow/tfjs` (2.0.4 +) yana kawar da mai ba da gudummawar ~ 800 MB mafi girma a baya — ragowar sawun shine transformers.js + onnxruntime-node runtimes, da samfurin TinyBERT (~ 57 MB) wanda aka sauke a farkon amfani (ba ta npm ba).

Kowane muhalli:

- **Dev/ `npm install`** an shigar da — ta atomatik sai dai idan kun wuce `--omit=optional` (ko `--no-optional`). Babu wani mataki da ake bukata.
- **Global npm (`npm i -g omniroute`) / tsaye** — yana gudanar da umarnin shigarwa da ke sama a cikin kundin fakitin da aka shigar, ko kuma sake shigar ba tare da barin deps na zaɓi ba.
- **Docker** — ƙara umarnin shigarwa a cikin hoton hoto da aka samo; hoton da aka buga yana jigilar siriri ta ƙira.
- **VPS (PM2)** shigar da — a cikin `node_modules` na app, sannan sake kunna aikin don haka ma'aikacin ya sake bincika ƙofar.
- **Raw Next standalone (`npm run build` → `.build/next/standalone/server.js`)** — da keɓaɓɓen binciken jiragen ruwa NEITHER ma'aikaci ko na zaɓin deps, don haka injin yayi shiru yana buɗewa. `scripts/build/colocate-standalone.mjs` ya sake yin amfani da duka biyun (ma'aikaci esbuild + na zaɓi na zaɓi-rufe cikin bishiyar kaɗaita); yana gudana ta atomatik ta hanyar ƙugiya `postbuild` npm bayan kowane ginin. Idepotent, kasa-laushi lokacin da deps ba ya nan.

**Tabbatar yana aiki:** tare da zaɓin LLMLingua, ainihin rubutun a zahiri yana raguwa (injin ya daina buɗewa), kuma buƙatun farko yana jawo zazzage samfurin zuwa `${DATA_DIR}/models/llmlingua`. Ƙofar da gangan ta bincika kawai `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` — sauran takwarorinsu ESM-kawai kuma `require.resolve` tana jefa su ko da a lokacin — don haka har yanzu ma'aikaci ya kasa buɗewa idan kowane ɗan'uwa ya ɓace da gaske a lokacin `import()`.

## Bututun da aka tara

Yanayin da aka haɗe yana gudanar da matakan bututun cikin tsari. Tsohuwar ita ce:

```txt
rtk -> caveman
```

Yi amfani da wannan don zama-kodi-wakili inda mai sauri ya haɗu da fitarwar umarni tare da ɗan adam ko mataimaki. RTK yana rage surutu kayan aiki da farko, sannan Caveman ya danne sauran yaren halitta.

Ana saita matakan bututun tare da `stackedPipeline` a cikin saitunan matsawa ko ta hanyar haɗakarwa.

Lokacin da duka injunan biyu suka rage nauyin da suka cancanta iri ɗaya, adadin tanadi:

```txt
combined = 1 - (1 - RTK savings) * (1 - Caveman input savings)
average  = 1 - (1 - 0.80) * (1 - 0.46) = 89.2%
range    = 1 - (1 - 0.60..0.90) * (1 - 0.46) = 78.4-94.6%
```

## MCP Samun damar Bishiyar Tace

Fitar da damar MCP -itace mai wayo shine Layer matsawa bayan aiwatarwa wanda ke gudana akan MCP** sakamakon kayan aiki** , ba akan tsokaci ko mahallin ba. Yana yin niyya ga isa ga bishiyar-bishiya da kayan aikin bincike da aka dawo da su ta kayan aikin kamar Playwright, amfani da kwamfuta, da sabar MCP mai bincike-atomatik.

### Abin da yake yi

1. **Cire surutu** — tana cire abubuwan shigar generic/text mara komai (`- generic:`, `- text: ""`)
2. **Rushewar 'yan uwantaka** — lokacin da ≥ `collapseThreshold` (tsoho 30) jeri-jere layi ne maimaituwar tsari, ya rurrushe su cikin layin `collapseKeepHead` na farko (default 10) + taƙaitaccen bayanin + layin `collapseKeepTail` na ƙarshe (tsoho 5)
3. **Ref adanawa** — `[ref=eXX]` anchors da Playwright/computer-use ke buƙata ba su taɓa taɓawa ba
4. **Tsatsa mai wuya** — idan har yanzu rubutun bayan rushewa ya wuce `maxTextChars` (tsoho 50,000), guntu tare da alamar kewayawa don haka wakilin zai ci gaba da aiki.

### Wurin inji

```txt

  collapseRepeated.ts ← sibling-collapse algorithm
  constants.ts        ← DEFAULT_MCP_ACCESSIBILITY_CONFIG
```

### tanadin da ake tsammani

## Abubuwan buƙatu / jagorar mai aiki (CCR &-deup-deup)

CCR (`ccr`) da `session-dedup` maye gurbin ko goge rubutu kuma su bar a** alama** a baya. Wannan sashe shine bayanin mai aiki don haɗa abokin ciniki sama, karanta alamomin biyu, da maidowa ko gano abin da ya ɓoye. An rufe injiniyoyin kowane injin a sama; wannan shine Layer "yadda zan gudanar da shi".

### Transport & wuraren ƙarshe

### Matakan ajiya (abin da ya tsira daga sake farawa)

CCR a** mataki biyu** kantin sayar da, ba a cikin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya-kawai:

- **Zafafan matakin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya** — per-block cap 2 MiB, per-principal 16 MiB, duniya 64 MiB. Shigar da tsoho zuwa 24 h TTL (mafi girman kwanaki 7). Cikakken dawo da MCP daga ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya an rufe shi a 256 KiB; Har yanzu ana iya samun duk wani abu mafi girma ta hanyar kewayon / grep.
- **Matsayin SQLite mai dorewa** (`ccr_blocks`, `COMPRESSION_CCR_DURABLE_STORE`, a kan ta tsohuwa) — yana goyan bayan matakin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya don haka toshe ya tsira daga korar LRU, sake farawa, ko dawo da saukowa a wani misali. Layin yana kiyaye TTL na toshe, don haka shingen da ya wuce TTL ɗinsa ya ɓace daga duka matakan biyu.

Masu gadi guda uku suna kiyaye iyakar — mai ɗorewa mai ɗorewa-kawai lokacin da:

1. toshe ya ƙare** 512 KB** (`MAX_DURABLE_BLOCK_BYTES`);
2. lokacin aiki shine lokacin gudu na girgije (babu diski na gida);
3. `COMPRESSION_CCR_DURABLE_STORE=false`.

Don haka "toshe ya ɓace" ba labarin sake kunnawa ba ne: toshe sub-512 KB a kan wani yanki na gida, lokacin da ba na girgije ba yana tsira daga sake kunnawa ta hanyar matakin mai ɗorewa har sai TTL ɗinsa ya ƙare. Duba [Maganar Muhalli](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) don ma'auni.

Wasan wasa ne na bi-biyu wanda ke daure da mai raba (`__`, `.`, `/`, `:`) — bai taba zama rukunonin rukunin igiya — ba don haka sunan da aka raba suna kamar `mcp__gateway__omniroute_ccr_retrieve` har yanzu ana kirgawa. `tools[]` da ba ya nan ko bai dace ba yana nufin ba a yi wa umarnin _ba_ allura ba, ta ƙira.

### Iyaka maidowa & pagination

Ana yanke sakamakon rubutun kayan aikin MCP a kusa da `mcpAccessibility.maxTextChars` (tsoho `50000`). Maido da `full` na toshe an sanya shi a 256 KiB —** babu cikakken maidowa mara iyaka** . Lokacin da toshe `chars=N` yana da girma, shafi shi maimakon ja da duka:

- Ka'idodin yatsan hannu:**`chars > 40000` → amfani da `mode`** , ba komai ya cika ba.
- `mode:"grep"` — regex `pattern` madaidaicin layi ta layi, har zuwa layukan daidaitawa 1,000 (`unique: true` drops repeats), mafi arha don "nemo layin da nake buƙata."
- `mode:"lines"` — kewayon layi na 1; yana buƙatar duka `start` da `end`.
- `mode:"head"` / `mode:"tail"` — first/last `n` layi.
- `mode:"stats"` — layi, hali, da byte ƙidaya ba tare da jiki ba.

Yi amfani da `mode:"lines"` tare da `start`/`end` don yin tafiya mai tsayi a cikin shafuka maimakon neman duka abu lokaci guda.

### Sunan kayan aiki

A halin yanzu ana kiran kayan aikin maido da `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` (prefix ɗin `omniroute_` ƙwararriyar aikin kwanan wata ce don karon sunan uwar garken). Sunan kayan aiki mai ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun kayan aiki da gyara don sakamakon MCP JSON da aka yanke tsakiyar kirtani ana bin diddigin su azaman masu biyo baya; wannan shafin yana tsara halin yanzu, da aka aika kawai.

### Zagayowar rayuwa

Streamable-HTTP zaman mara amfani da uwar garken girbe su; taga mara aiki shine kullun `MCP_SESSION_IDLE_MS` (wanda ake magana da suna, ba ƙima mai ƙima ba, don haka wannan shafin ba ya jujjuya lokacin da ya canza). Alamar zaman girbi mara aiki shine HTTP 404 tare da `Unknown Mcp-Session-Id` — maganin shine sake haɗawa ko sake farawa zaman,** ba** don juya maɓalli, canza iyaka, ko sake suna kayan aiki.

## Girman & ware

**Abun ciki ba a taɓa matsawa ba.** `open-sse/handlers/embeddings.ts` baya kiran duk wani injin matsawa — jikin request/response ya wuce kai tsaye zuwa ga mai zartarwa ba a taba ba. Wannan tsari ne a yau (masu haɗawa da kammala taɗi ba su da haɗin gwiwa), ba duba lokaci ba ne, amma yana nufin damuwa-hargitsi a cikin #8034 ba shi da wani fili a cikin hanyar haɗawa.

**tacewa Per-model/endpoint (#8034).** Don kammala taɗi, ma'aikaci na iya kiran ƙirar ids / `provider/model` na ƙira waɗanda ba za a taɓa matsawa ba — hanyar tsaro mai amfani idan an taɓa haɗawa da haɗawa kusa da hanyar haɗin gwiwa daga baya, kuma gabaɗaya yana da amfani ga kowane samfurin wanda ainihin abubuwan da ke da sauri na byte-for-byte (ƙaddara ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙima, prefixes masu ɗaukar hoto, da sauransu).

- Filin saiti: `exclusions?: string[]` akan tsarin matsi na duniya (`GET`/`PUT /api/settings/compression`), ya ci gaba ta hanyar sunan `key_value` matsawa (`src/lib/db/compression.ts`) — babu sabon tebur.
- Dashboard tab:** Dashboard → Compression → Keɓancewa** (`/dashboard/compression/exclusions`).
- Tsarin tsarin tsari: `*` ita ce kawai kati. Duk sauran metacharacter regex a cikin tsari ana tserewa kafin daidaitawa, don haka `gpt-5.6` ya dace da zaren zahiri kawai, ba za a taɓa `gpt-5x6` ba (ReDoS-lafiya, ɗaure, babu ƙididdige ƙididdiga). Samfurin sun yi daidai da shari'ar-rashin hankali da duka nau'ikan id ɗin ƙira da `provider/model` mai haɗawa — `gpt-5-6`, `openai/gpt-5-6`, da `openai/*` duk aiki, kuma `*` kaɗai ya keɓe kowane samfuri.
- Daidaitawa: `isCompressionExcluded()` / `normalizeCompressionExclusions()` a cikin `open-sse/services/compression/exclusions.ts`. `chatCore.ts` yana duba abin da aka cire daidai bayan warware saitunan matsawa,** kafin kowane injin ya kunna** , kuma yana kula da wasa daidai kamar matsawa da ake naƙasasshe a duniya — Ana yin rikodin tsallakewar ta hanyar `writeCompressionSkip(..., "excluded")` don hangen nesa na nazari.
- Default (jerin empty/absent ): kama da pre-#8034 hali — babu abin da aka ware.

## Iyakokin da aka sani

- **LLMLingua-2 (SLM) yana buƙatar deps na zaɓin tare.** Ma'aikacin yana gudana ne kawai a cikin ginin samarwa lokacin da takwarorinsu `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` + suka kasance tare cikin `dist/node_modules` (duba `scripts/build/colocateOptionals.mjs`, #4286). Ba tare da su injin yana buɗewa ba (yana dawo da ainihin rubutun). Ƙaddamar da ma'aikaci baya dogara da `import.meta.url` (ya mutu a cikin gungu na tsaye) — yana ɗagawa akan lokacin aiki cwd / `argv[1]`.
- **Fakitin harshen Caveman `de` / `fr` / `ja` bangare ne.** Suna jigilar `context` + `filler` + `structural` dokokin amma babu fakitin `dedup` / `ultra`, don haka ƙarfin `ultra` bai fi `full` ƙarfi ba ga waɗannan harsuna (suna amfani da ƙa'idodin kansu kawai — babu shiru-baya-baya ga ƙa'idodin `dedup`/`ultra` na Ingilishi, waɗanda zasu haɗa rubutun waje). `en` / `es` / `id` / `pt-BR` sun cika. Ana maraba da gudummawar `dedup.json` + `ultra.json` don fakitin ɓangarori.
- **Stacked telemetry yana lissafin injunan da suka matsa.** Matakan bututun da injina ya yi aiki amma ya samar da 0% tanadi ya dawo da `stats:null` don haka ba ya bayyana a cikin `engineBreakdown` — wanda ba ya bambanta da matakin da aka tsallake. Bambance "gudu, 0 %" daga "tsalle" yana buƙatar canji-samfurin kuma an jinkirta shi.
